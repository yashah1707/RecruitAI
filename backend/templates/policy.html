The university's own criteria

The UGC and AICTE Regulations set the minimum. Here the university may ask for more for a post. Each criterion is checked after the statutory assessment and shown beside it, to HR and to the interview panel.

A criterion here can only add to the statutory minimum. It never changes the statutory finding, and it cannot make eligible a candidate the Regulations find not eligible. A figure set below the statutory one has no effect, because the statutory check still applies. HR decides with both in view.
